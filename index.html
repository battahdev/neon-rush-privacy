Neon Rush

Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 27, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Neon Rush ("the game") handles information when you play the Android version of the game.

Developer: Ayoub Battah, independent developer. For privacy questions, contact the developer using the contact details published on the game's official app store listing.

Information and local game data

The game may store gameplay progress and preferences locally on your device, such as scores, unlocked items, and settings. This local data is used to operate game features. Uninstalling the game or clearing its storage may delete this data.

Advertising and Google AdMob

The game integrates Google AdMob to request and display advertisements, including rewarded ads that may offer an in-game revive. Advertising is provided by Google and may involve processing device or advertising-related information, such as an advertising identifier, IP address, app interaction information, diagnostics, and information used to prevent fraud or deliver and measure ads. The exact information processed can depend on your device, settings, region, and Google's services.

Google may process information as described in its own policies. Please review Google's Privacy Policy and Google AdMob information.

Consent and privacy choices

Where required, the game uses Google's consent tools to present privacy choices before requesting ads. Available choices and settings may depend on your location and applicable law. You can review or change certain Google advertising settings through your device settings and Google's ad settings. If consent cannot be established, the game may not request ads; core gameplay is intended to remain available.

Third-party services

The game uses third-party services from Google for advertising and consent management. Those services may process information under their own privacy terms. The developer does not control the independent privacy practices of third-party providers.

Children's privacy

The game is not intended to knowingly collect personal information directly from children. The developer does not knowingly ask players to submit personal information in the game. The developer should ensure the game's target audience and advertising settings are configured accurately in each app store and in AdMob.

Data retention and security

Gameplay data stored locally remains on your device until it is removed by you or by uninstalling the game. Data processed by third-party advertising services is subject to their retention and security practices. No method of data transmission or storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated as the game or its services change. The latest version will be published at this URL, with the updated date shown above.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact the developer through the contact information shown on the Neon Rush app store listing. Before public release, ensure a working contact email or another reliable contact method is available on that listing.
